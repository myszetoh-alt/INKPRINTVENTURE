Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Not sure where to begin? Here are a few quick answers.

Can I send artwork or printing files through WhatsApp?

Yes, you can start a WhatsApp conversation and discuss your job. Share your artwork, reference pictures, sizes and quantity in the chat so we can review the request.

Can you help design my name card, menu or poster?

Artwork and graphic design are among our services. Tell us what you're looking for, and we'll discuss the design options and any applicable charges.

Do you accept small printing jobs?

We provide everyday printing and photocopying services as well as larger projects. Minimum quantities may differ by product, so ask us about the item you need.

How long does printing take?

Turnaround depends on the product, order quantity, artwork readiness and current workload. Contact us to confirm an estimate before ordering.

Can you print customised sizes or materials?

Many jobs can be customised. Tell us the dimensions, material and intended use, and we'll advise what options are available.

Where is INK PRINT VENTURE located?

4397, Jalan Raja Uda, Taman Sin Tat, 12300 Butterworth, Pulau Pinang. Use the directions link below to plan your visit.

Need something printed today?

Call ahead to check options and turnaround. We're happy to discuss your project.
